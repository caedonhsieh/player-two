PLAYER TWO

You are not the hero. You are Player Two — the little wisp in the knight's helmet. Talk to him. That's your only control.

Room 1 — The Gate Room
Bram is standing still, waiting for instructions.

Objective

  • ☐ Find the key (it's behind the barred gate)
  • ☐ Unlock the east door and escape
  • ☐ Optional: pull the lever to open the gate

Bram's brain

Real LLM, running in your browser via WebLLM (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, ~1 GB). No server, no simulated hero — if the model can't load, the game won't start.

Try short, specific orders. Bram only remembers the last few things you said, takes “left” and “it” literally, and does the first step of a plan, then forgets the rest. If he's confused, he'll ask — answer him.
